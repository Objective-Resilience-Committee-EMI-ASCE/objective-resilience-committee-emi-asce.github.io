:::note[By invitation only]
For now, contributions are accepted by invitation only, and at least one author must be a member of EMI / the Objective Resilience Committee. If you would like to contribute, email name_example@uofexample.edu describing your case study or benchmark problem. If it is a fit, you will be invited to the repository and can follow the steps below.
:::

## Steps

1. **Copy the template.** Open [`TEMPLATE.md`](https://github.com/Objective-Resilience-Committee-EMI-ASCE/objective-resilience-committee-emi-asce.github.io/blob/main/TEMPLATE.md) in the repository and copy its contents.
2. **Create your file.** In the folder `src/content/docs/entries/`, choose **Add file → Create new file** and name it after your submission, for example `my-bridge-network.md` (lowercase, no spaces). Paste the template.
3. **Fill it in.** Complete every field at the top and every section below it. Write `N/A` where something genuinely does not apply. The comments inside the template explain each field.
4. **Add a figure (required).** Upload one figure of your system or results to `public/images/your-file-name/` and set `figure:` to its path, for example `/images/your-file-name/figure.png`. It is shown on the home page; also place it in the General Description of your entry as `![Caption](/images/your-file-name/figure.png)`.
5. **Open a pull request** to the `main` branch. Automatic checks confirm the file is complete. A committee member will review it and may ask for edits in the pull request discussion.
6. Once approved and merged, your entry appears on this site within a couple of minutes.

## Notes

- **Category and subcategory** must come from the lists in the template. ORC members decide where your entry appears in the left panel.
- **Data:** link to a public repository (GitHub, DesignSafe Data Depot, etc.). If the data cannot be fully shared, say what can be shared and under what conditions instead of leaving it blank.
- **Citation:** list any references you would like people to cite if they use your data or methods. They are shown in a box at the bottom of your entry. Use `[]` if you do not need one.
- Submissions are reviewed for completeness and clarity, not as peer-reviewed publications.
